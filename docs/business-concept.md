# Business Concept Document
**Status:** Public working draft · 26 November 2026  
**Company name:** To be selected by the founding process  
**Founder:** Pruden Jack Gideon — Original Founder / proposed CEO  
**Model:** Hybrid digital services, AI solutions and proprietary technology products

## 1. Executive concept
The proposal is to establish a remote-first digital solutions and technology company serving individuals, small and medium-sized businesses, startups and selected established organizations. The company will initially deliver practical digital services to build customer relationships and revenue, while developing the technical capability and market understanding needed to create its own scalable digital products.

The venture is at the founding stage. It is not represented as an already registered or fully operational company. The final name, legal form, ownership, board and executive appointments remain subject to discussion and formal approval.

## 2. Purpose
Help people and organizations use accessible digital technology to communicate better, operate more efficiently and develop useful products.

## 3. Vision
Build a trusted, innovative technology company that creates practical digital solutions and develops products with lasting value.

## 4. Mission
Combine skilled people, disciplined execution and responsible use of technology and AI to solve real customer problems, deliver quality work and grow sustainable digital products.

## 5. Strategic pillars
- **Digital services:** Websites, web applications, QR-code menus, digital business cards, branding, promotional content and digital marketing.
- **AI and automation:** AI assistants, workflow automation, integrations, intelligent business systems and AI-enabled customer experiences.
- **Proprietary products:** Research, validate, prototype and develop reusable applications, SaaS platforms and digital tools.

## 6. Initial target markets
1. Small and growing businesses needing digital presence or workflow improvements.
2. Restaurants, cafés, hotels and accommodation providers.
3. Startups, entrepreneurs and independent professionals.
4. Educational and community organizations where there is a clear use case.

## 7. Value proposition
A practical partner for accessible digital transformation: understand the need, propose an appropriately scoped solution, deliver transparently and support the customer after launch.

## 8. Operating principles
- Customer needs and validated demand come before technology for its own sake.
- Commitments must match actual team capacity.
- Work is documented, reviewed and tested before delivery.
- Customer data and credentials are handled responsibly.
- AI outputs are checked by people; AI tools are not employees or accountable decision-makers.
- The business keeps transparent records and separates company funds from personal funds.

## 9. Competitive approach
Compete through responsiveness, understandable pricing, practical solutions, reliable delivery and a growing portfolio of demonstrated work. Avoid claiming capabilities, certifications or results that have not been verified.

## 10. Founding-stage priorities
Evaluate potential founding candidates; agree on the name and legal pathway; validate the first service packages with prospective customers; build demonstrable examples; and establish basic financial, contractual and data-handling controls.

## 11. Success measures
Early measures include qualified customer conversations, proposals issued, paid projects delivered on scope, customer satisfaction, repeat work, documented delivery processes, cash-flow discipline and validated product opportunities.

## 12. Review
This document is a public working concept. It should be updated after the founding meeting and approved by the authorized founders before being treated as the company's adopted strategy.
