# Founders' Agreement — Discussion Draft
**Status:** Public discussion draft only · Not a contract · Not for signature  
**Date:** 26 November 2026

> This document outlines matters the founding candidates should negotiate. It does not create a partnership, employment relationship, directorship, shareholding or binding promise of equity. Obtain advice from a qualified Kenyan advocate and accountant before signing final documents or incorporating.

## 1. Founding intent
The participants intend to explore forming a hybrid digital solutions and technology business. Pruden Jack Gideon is the founder and is currently holding the CEO role on an interim basis during the formation process. This temporary arrangement does not determine the permanent leadership structure. The founders will discuss and agree leadership positions, responsibilities and decision-making arrangements during their initial meetings. The final governance structure is subject to agreement and applicable law.

## 2. Candidate assessment and admission
Expression of interest does not confer co-founder status. Candidates may be assessed on relevant skills, reliability, communication, availability, initiative and practical contribution. Any provisional appointment should have a defined scope and review date. Formal founder status must be expressly agreed in writing.

## 3. Contributions
Before formal admission, record each person's expected contribution: time, skills, equipment, intellectual property, cash, customer relationships or other resources. Distinguish promised contributions from contributions actually delivered. No participant should incur costs on behalf of the venture without prior approval.

## 4. Roles and responsibilities
Each role should have a written scope, accountable owner, expected outputs, time commitment, reporting line and review process. Titles do not replace measurable responsibilities.

## 5. Ownership and equity
No equity percentage or equal split is promised by this draft. Ownership discussions should consider sustained contribution, responsibilities, capital and other risk, intellectual property, time commitment, vesting, dilution and future financing. Any shares must be issued only through proper company approvals, constitutional documents and legally compliant instruments.

## 6. Decision-making
The founding group should define reserved matters, ordinary operational decisions, approval thresholds, quorum, voting method and deadlock resolution. Until a legal entity exists, no participant should represent that they can bind a company that has not yet been formed.

## 7. Intellectual property
The final agreement must identify pre-existing IP, project-created IP, open-source components, third-party licences and assignment or licensing terms. No participant should copy, disclose or transfer another person's work without permission. Client contracts must clearly address ownership and permitted portfolio use.

## 8. Confidentiality and data protection
Participants must protect customer information, credentials, personal data, proposals and non-public business information. Use least-privilege access, approved storage and secure credential handling. Public materials must not expose confidential terms or personal information without consent.

## 9. Compensation and expenses
There is no guaranteed salary at the initial stage. Project compensation, expense reimbursement, contractor fees and any future salaries must be agreed in writing before work or expenditure is undertaken. Compensation for work is distinct from dividends or shareholder distributions.

## 10. Financial controls
Record all income and expenditure. Direct project costs, statutory obligations and tax treatment must be considered before calculating net profit. The current proposal is to target 40% of net profit for business reinvestment; the use of the remaining 60% is deliberately subject to discussion and formal policy. This is not a guaranteed distribution.

## 11. Conflicts, conduct and customer relationships
Disclose conflicts of interest. Do not misuse company opportunities, client relationships, confidential information or assets. Agree reasonable conflict-resolution and non-solicitation terms only after legal review.

## 12. Departure and removal
The final agreement should define voluntary departure, incapacity, material breach, removal process, notice, handover, access revocation, treatment of unvested equity (if any), valuation and dispute resolution. No informal WhatsApp decision should substitute for legally required corporate procedures.

## 13. Disputes and governing law
The final document should specify applicable Kenyan law, internal escalation, mediation and any agreed formal dispute process, with advice from a Kenyan advocate.

## 14. Matters to resolve before signing
- Legal entity and registered name.
- Identity of actual founders and directors.
- Contributions and commitment period.
- Equity, vesting and transfer restrictions.
- Reserved matters and voting.
- IP assignment/licensing and confidentiality.
- Compensation, expenses and profit policy.
- Exit, deadlock and dispute procedures.
