# Organizational Structure & Leadership
**Status:** Proposed structure · Subject to candidate evaluation and founding approval

## 1. Founder and interim leadership
**Founder — Pruden Jack Gideon**  
Pruden is currently holding the CEO role on an interim basis during the formation stage and is coordinating the initial vision, formation process and early priorities. This is a temporary arrangement, not a permanent leadership appointment. The founders will discuss and agree each leadership position, responsibilities and decision-making arrangements during their initial meetings. No other leadership appointments are implied by this draft.

## 2. Proposed functions
| Function | Main responsibilities | Example outputs |
|---|---|---|
| Operations & Administration | Planning, documentation, coordination, records and process | Meeting notes, task plans, delivery tracker |
| Technology & Engineering | Web, app, infrastructure, integrations, testing and maintenance | Working products, deployments, technical documentation |
| Design & Creative | UI/UX, brand identity, visual systems and design assets | Design files, brand assets, prototypes |
| Marketing & Sales | Market research, outreach, proposals, partnerships and customer pipeline | Lead list, proposals, campaigns, sales reports |
| Media & Production | Photography, video, editing and content production | Client media, promotional assets |
| AI & Product Research | AI workflows, research, validation and prototypes | Research briefs, experiments, product prototypes |

## 3. Team size and flexibility
No fixed founding-team size or maximum has been set. The founder will first review the level of interest and evaluate the people who come forward. The initial number of members will be decided afterward, based on relevant skills, availability, commitment, business needs and the ability to assign clear responsibilities. The functions listed above are areas of work, not reserved seats or guaranteed executive offices. One person may cover multiple functions until workload justifies specialization.

## 4. Candidate information and evaluation
The founder will share a Google Sheet when ready to collect consistent information from interested candidates. Until that sheet is shared, no detailed application form is open. Assess practical skills, reliability, communication, availability, initiative and willingness to learn. Use short, relevant practical tasks and structured interviews where appropriate. Keep evaluation criteria consistent and explain that assessment does not guarantee admission or equity.

## 5. Reporting and accountability
Every active project should have one accountable owner, a scope, milestones, acceptance criteria and a reviewer. Team members should raise blockers early and record decisions. Customer-facing commitments require an authorized person to approve scope, timeline and price.

## 6. AI-supported work
ChatGPT, Claude, Grok and Gemini may assist with research, planning, drafting, coding and quality review. They are software tools, not employees, managers or accountable approvers. Human team members remain responsible for validation, security, licensing, privacy and final delivery.

## 7. Governance to finalize
The founders must decide whether a board is appropriate at the selected legal stage, identify statutory officers if required, define delegated authority, spending limits, voting and reserved matters, and adopt a conflict-of-interest policy.
