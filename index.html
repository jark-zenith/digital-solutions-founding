01 / WHY

Start with skills. Create value. Grow with purpose.

The founder & the idea

A practical beginning, with room to build something much bigger.

I’m Pruden Jack Gideon, a Kenya-based technology builder and the original founder behind this proposed venture. I’m still increasing my knowledge across ICT, software, AI and cloud technologies, with a practical, build-first approach to learning and problem-solving.

I started this initiative because I believe capable people can create meaningful digital solutions by combining their skills, curiosity and discipline. The goal is to build a company that first solves real customer problems, then uses what it learns to develop useful products of its own.

06 / Business model

Earn through delivery. Grow through products.

Our model combines project-based income with a longer-term path toward repeatable services and proprietary digital products.

Near-term revenue

Customer-funded work that solves defined business problems.

  • Website and application projects
  • Digital menus and business profiles
  • Content and marketing packages
  • AI workflow setup and integration
  • Maintenance and support agreements

Long-term growth

Build reusable capabilities and products from validated market needs.

  • Subscription software and SaaS
  • Reusable business tools
  • Proprietary applications
  • Product partnerships and licensing
  • Recurring support and service plans
Proposed financial principle

40% of net profit

Target allocation for business reinvestment, subject to cash-flow needs and an approved financial policy.

Illustrative allocation target — not 40% of gross customer payments.

60% to be discussed

The remaining net profit allocation will be discussed and agreed by the founding team. It is not an automatic equal distribution.

Financial discipline

Protect delivery. Reinvest with intention.

Project costs, taxes and other obligations must be accounted for before determining net profit. The proposed 40% reinvestment target applies to net profit, not gross revenue. The remaining 60% is deliberately left open for discussion.

All income and expenditure should be recorded, with spending approvals and periodic reporting. Compensation for work, retained earnings and shareholder dividends are distinct matters and must be handled under written policies and applicable law.

This is a proposed policy for discussion, not a final accounting, tax or legal determination.

09 / Frequently asked questions

Before you put your name forward.

Is the company already registered?

No. This is a proposed venture at the founding stage. The company name and legal structure are not yet finalized. Registration will be considered after the founding arrangements and practical requirements are agreed.

Does applying make me a co-founder?

No. The form records interest only. Candidates will be evaluated, invited to discussions and considered for provisional responsibilities. Co-founder status, directorship and shareholding require separate decisions and written agreements.

Will there be a salary immediately?

No guaranteed salary is available at the initial stage. Any paid project work, compensation or expense reimbursement must be agreed in advance and recorded transparently.

Do I need professional experience?

Not necessarily. Practical skills, learning ability, reliability, communication and willingness to complete agreed work are all relevant. Candidates should be honest about their current level.

How much time is expected?

The initial target is around 1–2 hours daily, with flexibility agreed realistically. Client deadlines may require additional coordinated effort, which should be discussed before accepting work.

How will ownership be decided?

No equity split has been promised. Ownership will be discussed only after contributions, responsibilities, commitment, risk and legal structure are understood, and then documented with appropriate professional advice.

What happens to the information I submit?

This first version opens an email draft on your device rather than storing applications in a database. Do not include identity documents, passwords or sensitive personal information. A secure application system should be configured before scaling recruitment.
